ABOUT

The voice that keeps your event moving

I'm Thomas Bolsens, a Belgium-based event host, moderator and master of ceremonies. Through To Be Announced, I host corporate events, sports events, award shows and live entertainment.

My job is simple: make sure your audience feels involved, your programme runs on time and your speakers and guests can shine. From a packed sports hall to a conference room, I adapt my energy to the room and to the people in it.
